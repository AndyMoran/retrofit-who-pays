A correction to my August retrofit business case.

I wrote that the Boiler Upgrade Scheme's flat £7,500 grant would cover most, or nearly all, of a right-sized heat pump in a well-insulated home. For owner-occupiers and private landlords that's true. For housing associations it isn't: the scheme is not open to social housing providers. That was reconfirmed after its April 2026 refresh, and it applies to the new £2,500 air-to-air grant as well.

For social landlords the heat pump route is the Warm Homes: Social Housing Fund instead: £7,500 per home base, plus £7,500 for off-gas-grid low-carbon heating, 50:50 match funded, and spent by 31 March 2028.

The underlying finding, that insulating first lets you buy a smaller and cheaper heat pump and battery, still stands. The grant that I said would pay for it doesn't apply to the audience I wrote it for. I caught it while cross-checking against later work, and I'm publishing a fuller update this week.
